Cyber Threat Library

Explore common cyber threats, recognize warning signs, and learn practical ways to protect your mobile devices and personal information.

🛡️ Cyber Threat Categories

📱

SMS Phishing

Fake text messages designed to trick users into clicking malicious links or revealing sensitive data.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Urgent messages, unknown links, fake verification, account suspension threats.
What is it?
Attackers send fraudulent SMS messages pretending to be banks, delivery services, or trusted organizations.

Stay Safe:
Avoid unexpected links. Verify messages through the organization's official website or app.
📲

Fake Apps

Malicious applications that imitate legitimate apps to steal information or perform harmful actions.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Unknown developer, excessive permissions, suspicious download source.
What is it?
Fake applications imitate genuine banking, social media, security, or utility applications.

Stay Safe:
Install apps from trusted stores and check developer information and requested permissions.
🔳

QR Scams

Fraudulent QR codes that redirect users to malicious websites or fake payment pages.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Unknown QR codes, unexpected payment requests, suspicious websites after scanning.
What is it?
Attackers may use QR codes to redirect victims to phishing websites or fraudulent payment pages.

Stay Safe:
Check the destination URL before entering passwords, OTPs, or payment information. Remember, receiving money generally does not require entering your UPI PIN.
🔐

OTP Scams

Social engineering attacks where criminals try to obtain one-time passwords from victims.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Someone asking for OTP, urgent verification, fake customer-care calls.
What is it?
Attackers may impersonate banks, support agents, or service providers to obtain authentication codes.

Stay Safe:
Never share OTPs, passwords, or authentication codes with callers or messages.
🔗

Malicious Links

Deceptive links that lead to fraudulent websites, credential theft pages, or harmful downloads.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Misspelled domains, shortened URLs, unexpected attachments, unusual login pages.
What is it?
Criminals distribute unsafe links through texts, email, social media, and messaging apps.

Stay Safe:
Check the domain carefully. Open important services using their official apps or saved bookmarks.
🦠

Malware

Malicious software created to damage systems, steal information, or disrupt operations.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Unknown downloads, strange pop-ups, unusual device behavior.
Common Types:
Viruses, Trojans, spyware, worms, and ransomware.

Stay Safe:
Keep your operating system updated, use trusted applications, and avoid suspicious downloads.
🧠

Social Engineering

Psychological manipulation used to convince people to reveal information or perform unsafe actions.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Pressure, fear, authority claims, emotional manipulation.
What is it?
Attackers manipulate people into making security mistakes instead of attacking technology directly.

Stay Safe:
Verify identities independently and never make security decisions under pressure.
👤

Account Security

Practices that protect online accounts from unauthorized access and account takeover.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Unexpected login alerts, password reset requests, unfamiliar devices.
What should you do?
Review login activity and remove unknown devices.

Stay Safe:
Use unique passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, and secure recovery options.
🔑

Password Attacks

Attempts to obtain passwords through guessing, credential theft, or automated attacks.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Repeated login attempts, breached credentials, suspicious login locations.
Common Attacks:
Brute force, credential stuffing, and password spraying.

Stay Safe:
Use long, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication. A password manager can help.
📡

Public Wi-Fi Risks

Unsafe or impersonated wireless networks can expose users to privacy and security risks.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Duplicate network names, no password protection, unexpected sign-in pages.
What is the risk?
Attackers may create look-alike hotspots or try to intercept poorly protected connections.

Stay Safe:
Confirm the network name with staff, use HTTPS, and avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted networks.
🔵

Bluetooth Attacks

Risks involving insecure Bluetooth connections, unexpected pairing, or vulnerable devices.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Unknown pairing requests, unfamiliar devices, unexpected connection prompts.
What is it?
Vulnerable devices or unsafe pairing practices can expose nearby devices to security risks.

Stay Safe:
Turn Bluetooth off when unnecessary, reject unknown pairing requests, and install device updates.
🕵️

Spyware

Software that secretly collects information about a user's activity or device.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Suspicious permissions, unfamiliar apps, unexplained data or battery use.
What is it?
Spyware can monitor activity and collect information without appropriate user awareness or consent.

Stay Safe:
Review app permissions, remove untrusted apps, and keep device security protections enabled.
🔒

Ransomware

Malware that can lock or encrypt files and demand payment to restore access.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Files suddenly inaccessible, ransom notes, unexpected file extensions.
What is it?
Ransomware can disrupt access to personal or organizational data and may threaten data exposure.

Stay Safe:
Maintain offline or protected backups, update software, and never open suspicious attachments. If affected, seek trusted incident-response help.
📶

SIM Swap Fraud

Fraudsters try to transfer a victim's phone number to a SIM they control.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Sudden loss of mobile service, unexpected SIM-change messages, account alerts.
What is it?
Criminals may impersonate a subscriber to persuade a mobile provider to transfer their number.

Stay Safe:
Contact your mobile provider immediately if service unexpectedly stops. Secure important accounts with stronger authentication options.
📞

Call Scams

Fraudulent callers impersonate trusted people or organizations to steal money or information.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Threats, urgent payment demands, requests for OTPs, PINs, or remote access.
What is it?
Vishing is voice-based phishing in which attackers manipulate people over phone calls.

Stay Safe:
Hang up and independently call the official organization using a verified contact number.
🛡️

App Permissions

Apps may request access to sensitive device features that are unnecessary for their purpose.

⚠ Warning Signs:
Flashlight apps requesting contacts, unexplained microphone or location access.
What should you check?
Review camera, microphone, location, contacts, SMS, and accessibility permissions.

Stay Safe:
Grant only necessary permissions and periodically review access in your phone's settings.
